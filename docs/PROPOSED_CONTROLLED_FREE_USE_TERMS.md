# PROPOSAL — CryNexLunaToolkit Controlled Free-Use Terms (v0.1)

**Status: DRAFT FOR RIGHTS-HOLDER / LEGAL REVIEW. NOT AN EFFECTIVE LICENSE.**  
**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Pierreg99/CryNexLunaToolkit

This document proposes a controlled, no-fee use model for the independently distributed "Luna's First Project Pack", "Nexus Luna Toolkit Classic", the Launchpad, and the Creator Kit. **It does not itself grant rights.** Until the authorized rightsholder approves and publishes final terms, existing permissions and applicable law govern. Do not present the draft as an open-source license.

## 1. Intended permission, once adopted

Subject to the rights inventory and exceptions below, the authorized copyright owner would grant each recipient a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, non-transferable permission** to:

- Download and run the packaged browser tools on their own devices, offline or through a self-hosted private local environment.
- Read the supplied documentation and prompts for personal study, instruction, and internal work.
- Keep a backup, and adapt the source or templates **for personal, educational, or internal organizational use**, including internal business use, without distributing the adapted project.
- Create and retain their **own output**, including task boards, draft emails, formatted JSON, goals, prompts and new documents. These terms are not intended to claim copyright in independently authored user content.

The no-fee grant would not require registration, a subscription, or a payment. External AI services chosen by users remain subject to their separate terms and may cost money.

## 2. Permissions NOT included

Except as permitted by mandatory law, separate written authorization, or a file-specific third-party license:

- Do not publish, redistribute, sell, sublicense or repackage the software, source files, ZIP archives, documentation, prompt collections or derivative versions to third parties.
- Do not provide the toolkit or a modified copy as a public website, SaaS, hosted service, app-store listing, white-label product, or paid bundle.
- Do not extract the supplied artwork, wallpapers, logos or visual assets for use in other public or commercial works, merchandise, model training datasets or asset packs.
- Do not imply endorsement by the project, its contributors, GPAI, OECD or other organizations.

The above restrictions concern the **toolkit itself**, not user-created content or ordinary legitimate use of resulting outputs.

## 3. File-type scope (proposed)

| Material | Candidate scope of the controlled grant | What remains restricted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source code (e.g. `index.html`, `app.js`, `styles.css`, `classic/`, `starter-project/`, `scripts/`, `data/`) | Run, inspect and modify privately or internally | Public redistribution, software resale, public hosted copies, sublicensing |
| Authored text (e.g. `README.md`, `docs/`, `freebie-creator-kit/`, `.agents/skills/`) | Read, copy into private notes, adapt prompts for internal work | Reposting the supplied documents or collections as one's own downloadable product |
| Project images (e.g. `assets/*.png`, `assets/*.webp`, `assets/*.svg`) | Display as part of the authorized local toolkit | Independent redistribution, repurposing or merchandising |
| Packaged ZIPs (`downloads/`) | Download and use the contents locally | Rehosting, repackaging or selling the archives |
| User-entered content and independently created outputs | User keeps applicable rights in own material | No additional project-claimed rights |
| Third-party contributions/assets | Only the permissions supported by their own licenses or valid grants | This proposal cannot relicense third-party material |

**Exceptions:** Existing third-party licenses, non-waivable statutory rights, GitHub platform terms, and existing author agreements prevail where applicable. No trademark license is offered. Where material is not owned or validly sublicensable by the issuer, it **must not** be included under this grant.

## 4. Conditions (proposal)

Preserve copyright, attribution and rights notices in any permitted internal copies. Do not remove third-party acknowledgments. The authorization would terminate for a material breach after notice and a reasonable opportunity to remedy where applicable; existing permitted rights and mandatory rights are not altered retroactively by a future change of terms.

Materials would be provided "as is", without a promise of suitability or security, **only to the extent legally permitted**. Nothing in the terms excludes liability or consumer protections that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 5. GitHub visibility and prior permissions

Public GitHub repositories remain viewable and forkable within GitHub under GitHub's Terms of Service. This proposal is **not** an attempt to override that platform permission. It also cannot revoke valid licenses already granted on earlier versions or rights independently granted by third parties.

This is a *source-available, controlled free-use proposal*, **not** a license approved by the Open Source Initiative and **not** an open-source project license.

## 6. Approval and publication gate

**Do not rename this document to LICENSE or merge it as an effective rights grant automatically.** Before activation:

1. Establish the rights holder for each covered source code file, document, AI-assisted image and third-party asset; retain proof.
2. Confirm whether historical releases, past contributors and upstream licenses impose obligations.
3. Have the owner explicitly accept the exact free-use scope (particularly internal business use) and get appropriate legal review for EU/German applicability and consumer-facing distribution.
4. Decide and insert accurate issuer name, copyright year(s), contact channel and effective date into final documents.
5. Put the adopted full text and a concise German summary in every affected downloadable ZIP and accessible website entry, and test the actual extracted packages.
6. Update the website/README only after final approval, verify packaging reproducibility and browser quality, then merge through the normal review process.

This draft intentionally avoids inventing an owner identity, an email address, or a release date.
